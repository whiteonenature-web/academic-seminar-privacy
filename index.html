SEMINAR

Law, Society, and the Freedom of Belief

This academic seminar examines the legal protection of religious expression by comparing the constitutional frameworks of South Korea and Belgium. It considers the distinction between the legal guarantee of freedom and its practical exercise in society.

The discussion focuses on legal protection, legitimate limits, proportionality, and the relationship between religious expression and the rights and freedoms of others.

Academic Scope

  • Constitutional provisions
  • Constitutional and Supreme Court cases
  • European Court of Human Rights
  • Court of Justice of the European Union
  • Practical legal questions
